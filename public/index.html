FAQ

questions we get on calls

what exactly do i get for $1,499

A custom homepage concept in Figma, hero plus 3 sections, a scroll animation, and our take on the direction your redesign should go. Delivered in 7 days.

what if i do not move forward with the full website

You keep the concept and can build it however you want. The $1,499 is credited if you upgrade within 60 days. Finding out on day 7 beats finding out on week 4.

are revisions included in the concept

No. It is a fixed concept, 1 direction, one shot. Revisions and evolution happen inside the full project, and that is priced separately.

can i give input before you start

You can share context and we read everything you send. But we do not ask for moodboards or brand kits. You are paying for our call on the direction, not for assembling yours.

will i be able to edit the website myself afterwards

Yes. We build in Webflow or Framer with a real CMS. Your marketing person can swap copy, add a post and publish without calling us. We lock the layout rules early so a text change on desktop does not break tablet.

what happens if you go quiet for a week

We do not. You get a private Slack channel with the people doing the work, weekly updates, and a status line on Friday whether or not there is good news. Delays are survivable, silence is what kills projects.

is an ai doing the design

No. We vibe code the build, which is why a month is enough for a full website. The direction, the layout and the taste are ours. You can see that in the concepts above.

who owns the files

You do. Figma, the Webflow or Framer project, fonts, everything handed over at the end.
